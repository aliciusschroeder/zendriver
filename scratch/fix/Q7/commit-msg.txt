Skip already removed domains when pruning in _register_handlers

The final loop of Connection._register_handlers removes the stale
auto-enabled domains it found in its snapshot of enabled_domains. When
the pass also has to enable a new domain, the awaited domain.enable()
send runs _register_handlers again through aopen(), and that nested
pass already removes the stale domain. The outer pass then called
list.remove() on it a second time and raised ValueError out of send(),
so the user's command was never sent. Only remove domains that are
still present.

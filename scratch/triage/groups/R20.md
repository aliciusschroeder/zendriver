### A10.4: Element.send_keys drops newlines from str text ("\n" and "\r" are sent as char "\n", which Chrome ignores), so multi-line text loses line breaks and "query\n" does not submit
- where (may drift): zendriver/core/keys.py:KeyEvents.conv_to_str (via _get_key_and_text, CHAR path):167
- finder severity: wrong-result
- claim: `await textarea.send_keys('line1\nline2\rline3')` gives the value 'line1line2line3'. `await search_input.send_keys('query\n')` never submits the form. Element.send_keys(str) calls KeyEvents.from_text(text, KeyPressEvent.CHAR), which maps '\n' and '\r' to SpecialKeys.ENTER. _get_key_and_text then turns that into conv_to_str(ENTER), which returns '\n'. Chrome treats Input.dispatchKeyEvent(type='char', text='\n') as keypress charCode 10: it inserts nothing and does not trigger implicit form submission. Only '\r' acts as Enter. The class's own SPECIAL_KEY_CHAR_MAP maps ENTER to '\r'. send_keys' docstring even tells users that sending \n works wonders. Oddly, CRLF works: '\r\n' is a single grapheme, so it falls through to a raw char '\r\n'.
- finder evidence (unverified): Probes /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-zendriver/b2021b39-94e6-5220-9e66-7f33c0314a80/scratchpad/detect-A10/probe_keys.py and probe_keys2.py on Chromium: `C 'abc\n' submits: 0`, `C2 'abc\r' submits: 0`, `D2 textarea 'a\nb' value: "ab"`, `send_keys textarea 'line1\nline2\rline3' -> "line1line2line3"`. Raw char '\r' gave `textarea value: "a\nb"` and `input form submits: 1`, while raw char '\n' gave "ab" and 0. Payload printed for send_keys('\n'): `{'type_': 'char', 'text': '\n', ...}`.
- finder repro idea: Page with <form onsubmit> plus an <input> and a <textarea>. Call send_keys('abc\n') and check the submit count, then send_keys('a\nb') and read the textarea value. Compare with raw dispatch_key_event(type_='char', text='\r').
